1. Problem Statement
1.1 Seamless Report Submission & Visibility
The Cell Leaders are expected to submit a report after every Sunday cell meeting. In practice,
submission is inconsistent, and there is no dashboard that shows Section Leaders, Area/Zonal
Coordinators, District Coordinators, or Regional Coordinators which cells reported and which
did not. Leaders currently have to chase this information manually (calls, WhatsApp, verbal
check-ins), which is slow, unreliable, and not auditable.
1.2 First-Timer / New-Convert Follow-Up Visibility
The MSU unit captures first-timers and new converts and assigns each one to a Cell Leader for
follow-up and integration. Cell Leaders are not reliably seeing these assignments, and once
assigned, there is no visibility into whether contact was actually made. This creates a real risk
that guests and new converts fall through the cracks between their first visit and integration
into a cell
1.3 Why This Matters
● Pastoral risk: unreported cells and uncontacted first-timers mean people in need
of care go unnoticed.
● Leadership blind spot: coordinators at every level cannot manage what they
cannot see.
● No accountability trail: there is no historical record of who submitted, who
approved, who was contacted, or when.

-- 1 of 21 --

● Manual chasing is not scalable across a structure that can span hundreds of
cells.
1.4 Hypothesis
● If Cell Leaders can submit Sunday reports through both a web app and
WhatsApp, then overall report submission rates will increase.
● If Section, Area, Zonal, District, and Regional leaders have a real-time
compliance dashboard instead of manually chasing reports, then the time it
takes to identify non-reporting cells will drop from days to minutes, and
non-submission will be caught and addressed faster.
● If Cell Leaders receive both an email and an in-app/WhatsApp notification when
a first-timer or new convert is assigned to them, then the percentage of
assignments that go unnoticed will drop significantly, since leaders won't be
solely dependent on checking email
● If MSU and upline coordinators can see real-time contact status for every
assigned first-timer/new convert, then follow-up completion rates within 7 days
will improve, because uncontacted guests can be flagged and reassigned before
they're lost.
● If automated reminders and escalation are introduced for both report submission
and first-timer follow-up, then chronic non-compliance (repeated missed reports
or uncontacted guests) will decline, since accountability shifts from manual
chasing to a systematic, visible process.

5. Proposed Process Flow
The following workflows are the proposed workflows (see the attached Daystar Alimosho
Small Group Solution process-flow document). These are new workflows to be built, not a
description of how things currently work. This proposed flow forms the functional backbone
that the rest of this PRD builds on.
5.1 Cell/Section/Area/Zone/District/Region Onboarding
1. The Super Admin signs in to the web app and downloads a CSV template.
2. The template is shared with the Zonal Coordinators (or designated data-entry
owner for that arm), who fill in Region, District, Zone, Area, Section, and Cell
details.
3. The completed CSV is submitted back to the Super Admin.
4. The Super Admin uploads the CSV, and the system bulk-creates the Region,
District, Zone, Area, Section, and Cell records and their hierarchy relationships.
5.2 Leader Signup (Magic Link) - All Levels
The same pattern applies at every level. Cell Leader, Section Leader, Area Coordinator,
Zonal Coordinator, District Coordinator, and Regional Coordinator:
1. The leader receives a magic-link email tied to the email address captured during
onboarding.
2. The leader opens the link, sets a password, and completes onboarding (profile
details).
3. The leader signs in to the platform to perform their role-specific actions.
5.3 Cell Leader Sunday Report Submission - Web App
1. The Cell Leader signs in to the web app.

-- 5 of 21 --

2. On successful authentication, the Cell Leader clicks “Submit Cell Report.”
3. A report form opens; the Cell Leader completes and submits it.
4. The Section Leader receives an email to approve the report. If the Section
Leader is unavailable, the Area/Zonal Coordinator approves it instead.
5.4 Cell Leader Sunday Report Submission - WhatsApp
1. The Cell Leader opens WhatsApp and starts a chat with the Daystar reporting
bot.
2. The Cell Leader enters the number/code associated with report submission.
3. The Cell Leader enters the required details and submits; the bot confirms
receipt.
4. As with the web flow, the Section Leader receives an email to approve; if
unavailable, the Area/Zonal Coordinator approves.
5.5 Area/Zonal Coordinator Report Submission - Web App
1. The Area/Zonal Coordinator signs in and, on successful authentication, clicks
“Submit Cell Report.”
2. A report form opens; the Coordinator completes and submits based on the cell
selected
5.6 MSU First Timer / New Convert Assignment - Web App
1. The MSU team signs in to the web app.
2. On successful authentication, the MSU team member navigates to “New Entry.”
3. They select whether the person is a “First Timer” or a “New Convert” from a
dropdown.
4. They complete the form, assign a Cell Leader based on the location of the first
timer/ new convert and cell location, and submit.
5. The assigned Cell Leader is notified by email and can view the details on the
web app.
6. The Cell Leader signs in to view the full details and contacts the guest/new
convert.

7. If contact is made, the Cell Leader updates the record as “Contacted” with
comments. If the Section/Area/Zonal Coordinator has to step in to make contact
instead, that should also be logged against the record.
6. Scope
6.1 In Scope
● Compliance dashboards for Sunday cell report submission, rolled up across the full
3-by-3 hierarchy (Cell → Section → Area → Zone → District → Region).
● Real-time “submitted / not submitted / pending approval / approved” status per cell, per
Sunday.
● First-timer/new-convert assignment visibility and contact-status tracking, with roll-up
dashboards for MSU and upline leaders.
● Automated reminders (emails) to Cell Leaders who have not submitted, and to
approvers with pending approvals.
● Escalation rules when reports or follow-ups remain outstanding past a configurable
threshold.
● Exportable compliance reports (CSV/PDF) by date range and hierarchy level.
● Building the CSV bulk-onboarding, magic-link signup, and multi-channel (Web +
WhatsApp) report submission flows

10. High-Level Data Model
Core entities the platform must support (attributes indicative, not exhaustive):
• Region, District, Zone, Area, Section, Cell. Hierarchical entities, each with a parent
reference matching the 3-by-3 structure.
• User/Leader: linked to exactly one hierarchy node and a role (Cell Leader, Section Leader,
Area Coordinator, Zonal Coordinator, District Coordinator, Regional Coordinator, MSU,
Super Admin).
• SundayReport: cell, service date, submitted-by, submission channel (Web/WhatsApp),
submitted-at, status (submitted / pending approval / approved/rejected), approved-by,
approved-at.
• FirstTimer/NewConvert: type (first timer / new convert), captured-by (MSU), assigned Cell
Leader, assignment date, contact status (not contacted/contacted / unable to reach),
contact comments, contact date, reassignment history.
• Notification Log: recipient, type (reminder/escalation/assignment), channel, sent-at,
related entity (report or first-timer record).
